DAYFLOW – HR POLICY AND USER GUIDE
Knowledge file for Dayflow Assistant (AI HR helper inside the Dayflow HRMS).

Part A describes how the Dayflow app actually works.
Part B is company policy. Items marked [EDIT] are sample values — change them to your company's real rules.

##################################################
PART A – HOW TO USE DAYFLOW
##################################################

==================================================
A1. ABOUT DAYFLOW
==================================================
Dayflow is a Human Resource Management System (HRMS) for managing attendance, leave, payroll and employee profiles.

There are two roles:
- Employee
- Admin (HR)

Employee sidebar pages: Dashboard, My Profile, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Settings.
Admin sidebar pages: Dashboard, Employees, Attendance, Leave Requests, Payroll, Reports, Settings.

==================================================
A2. SIGN UP AND SIGN IN
==================================================
How to create an account:
1. Open Sign Up.
2. Enter your Employee ID (for example EMP-001), full name and email.
3. Click "Send OTP". A 6-digit OTP is sent to your email (check spam if you don't see it).
4. Enter the OTP and click "Verify". You will see "Email verified".
5. Now set your password and confirm it, then click "Create Account".
6. Sign in with your email and password.

Notes:
- The OTP expires in 5 minutes. You can request a new one after 60 seconds.
- After 5 wrong OTP attempts, request a new OTP.
- New accounts are created as Employee. Admin (HR) access is given by the company.
- After signing in, employees go to the Employee Dashboard and admins go to the Admin Dashboard.

Password rules:
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 special character

To change your password: open Settings → Change Password. Enter your current password, then the new password twice.

==================================================
A3. ATTENDANCE
==================================================
How to mark attendance:
1. Open the Attendance page from the sidebar.
2. Click "Check In" when you start work.
3. Click "Check Out" when you finish work.
4. After checking out, the day shows as "Day Complete" with your total hours.

Rules in Dayflow:
- You can check in only once per day.
- You must check in before you can check out.
- You can check out only once per day.
- Total hours = time between Check In and Check Out.
- If you work less than 4 hours, the day is marked "Half Day".
- If you work 4 hours or more, the day is marked "Present".

Attendance statuses: Present, Absent, Half Day, Leave, Holiday, Weekend.

The Attendance page shows your monthly summary: present days, absent days, half days, leave days and total hours worked. The Employee Dashboard shows hours worked this week.

Forgot to check in or check out? Employees cannot edit attendance themselves. Contact HR/Admin — the admin can manually mark or correct your attendance from the admin Attendance page.

==================================================
A4. LEAVE
==================================================
Leave types available in Dayflow:
- Paid Leave
- Sick Leave
- Casual Leave
- Unpaid Leave
- Maternity Leave
- Paternity Leave

How to apply for leave:
1. Open the Leave page ("Leave & Time Off") from the sidebar.
2. Click "Apply for Leave".
3. Select the leave type.
4. Choose the Start Date and End Date.
5. Write the reason in "Reason / Remarks" (maximum 500 characters).
6. Submit the request.

Rules in Dayflow:
- The start date cannot be in the past.
- The end date must be the same as or after the start date.
- You cannot apply for dates that overlap with another pending or approved leave request.
- Total days are counted including both the start and end date (for example, 10th to 12th = 3 days).

Leave status:
- Pending – waiting for admin review
- Approved – accepted by admin
- Rejected – not accepted by admin
- Cancelled – cancelled by you

The admin can add a comment when approving or rejecting. You can see it on your leave request.

Cancelling leave: You can cancel a leave request only while it is still Pending. Once approved or rejected, contact HR to make changes.

Leave balance: The Leave page shows Total Allowed, Used, Remaining and Pending requests.
- Each employee gets 24 leave days per calendar year in total.
- "Used" counts only approved leave in the current year.
- Remaining = 24 − Used.

==================================================
A5. PAYROLL AND SALARY SLIPS
==================================================
- Open the Payroll page to see your Latest Net Salary, Latest Gross Salary and all your Salary Slips.
- Click "Download" on any salary slip to save it.
- Salary slips are created by the admin from Payroll Management, one per employee per month.

Salary slip parts:
- Earnings: Basic, HRA, Allowances, Bonus, Overtime
- Deductions: Tax, Insurance, Provident Fund (PF), Other
- Gross Salary = total earnings (before deductions)
- Net Salary = Gross Salary − total deductions
- Working days: total working days, present days, absent days and leave days in that month

Salary slip status:
- Draft – being prepared
- Generated – slip is ready
- Paid – salary has been paid

==================================================
A6. MY PROFILE
==================================================
The My Profile page has these sections:
- Basic Information (name, phone, date of birth, gender, profile picture)
- Address
- Emergency Contact (name, relationship, phone)
- Work Details (department, designation/position, date of joining, employment type, reporting manager, work location)
- Salary Structure (basic, HRA, allowances, deductions, net salary, currency)
- Documents (resume, ID proof, offer letter, etc.)

Employment types: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern.

==================================================
A7. NOTIFICATIONS AND SETTINGS
==================================================
- The bell icon in the top bar shows your notifications (for example, leave approved or rejected).
- In Settings → Notification Preferences you can turn on or off: email notifications, leave updates, attendance reminders and payroll alerts.

==================================================
A8. FOR ADMINS (HR)
==================================================
- Employees: view and manage all employee profiles.
- Attendance: see everyone's attendance by day or month, filter by department, and manually mark attendance.
- Leave Requests: see pending, approved and rejected requests; approve or reject with a comment.
- Payroll: generate monthly salary slips for employees (select employee, month, year, earnings and deductions).
- Reports: view analytics on attendance, leave and payroll.

PART B – COMPANY POLICY
##################################################

==================================================
B1. WORKING HOURS [EDIT]
==================================================
- Working days: Monday to Friday.
- Office hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, including a 1 hour lunch break.
- A full working day is expected to be about 8 hours. Less than 4 hours is a Half Day in Dayflow.

==================================================
B2. LEAVE POLICY [EDIT]
==================================================
- Total leave allowed: 24 days per calendar year (this matches the Dayflow leave balance).
- Casual Leave: apply at least 1 day in advance.
- Paid Leave: apply at least 7 days in advance.
- Sick Leave: a medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 continuous days. Share it with HR.
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks, as per Indian law.
- Paternity Leave: 5 working days.
- Unpaid Leave: only with HR approval; salary is deducted for these days.
- Unused leave does not carry forward to the next year.

==================================================
B3. SALARY [EDIT]
==================================================
- Salary is paid on the last working day of every month.
- Salary slips are available on the Payroll page by the 5th of the next month.
- For any mistake in your salary slip, contact HR within 7 days.

==================================================
B4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS [EDIT]
==================================================
Fixed national holidays:
- Republic Day – 26 January
- Independence Day – 15 August
- Gandhi Jayanti – 2 October

Festival holidays (Holi, Diwali, Eid, Christmas, etc.) are announced by HR at the start of each year.
